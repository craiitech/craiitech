# OPERATIONAL PLAN & KPI SYSTEM (FORM QAO-01-025)

## Master Implementation Blueprint & Architecture

**Document ID:** RSU-OP-IMP-2026  
**Associated Standard:** Romblon State University ISO 21001:2018 EOMS & Form QAO-01-025

---

## Architecture Summary

This implementation provides:

1. **Dual-Mode System Toggle (Admin Settings):**
   - Allows the Admin to switch seamlessly between **Legacy Google Drive Link** mode and **Interactive Form QAO-01-025 Data Entry** mode.
2. **VP Institutional Objectives Cascading:**
   - Vice Presidents (VPAA, VPAF, VPREDI) publish annual strategic quality objectives.
   - Unit Heads select from these institutional objectives when formulating operational tasks.
3. **Interactive Form QAO-01-025 Workspace:**
   - Units draft, edit, and organize operational plan rows in Draft mode.
   - Submits to VP / Campus Director for review and digital signature.
4. **Immutability & Locking Engine:**
   - Once approved, baseline objectives, tasks, budgets, and success indicators are **locked and cannot be edited or deleted** by the unit.
   - In Final Cycle, progress accomplishment and remarks unlock for reporting year-end results with documentary evidence (Google Drive MOV links).
5. **Print & PDF Generator for Form QAO-01-025:**
   - Auto-generates the exact ISO-compliant document with official RSU headers, TÜV Rheinland ISO 21001 mark, and digital signature blocks.
6. **Automated KPI Engine Integration:**
   - Computes unit attainment scores, schedule adherence, and feeds metrics directly into institutional executive dashboards.
